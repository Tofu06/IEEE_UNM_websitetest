WHO WE ARE

About IEEE UNM SB

IEEE UNM SB stands for the Institute of Electrical Electronics Engineering University of Nottingham Student Branch.

We are an academic society that organises a variety of events and activities throughout the academic terms at the University of Nottingham Malaysia.

OUR AIM

Technology for the benefit of humanity.

Aim

Aligned with the mission of IEEE, IEEE UNM SB aims to foster technological innovation and excellence for the benefit of humanity.

OUR OBJECTIVES

What we strive to achieve.

01

Networking

Provide a networking platform for electrical and electronics engineers.

02

Professionalism

Nurture a mindset of professionalism and innovative thinking.

03

Technical & Soft Skills

Sharpen the technical and soft skills of members to better prepare them for industry.

04

Volunteerism

Nurture volunteerism and support impactful humanitarian activities at community or local level.

OUR VISION & MISSION

Growing together through engineering.

Vision

To create a rapport community that fosters great teamwork and inspires lifelong learning in the field of Electrical and Electronic Engineering.

Mission

IEEE UNM SB organises participant-centred events and activities that benefit participants in their professional and personal development.
